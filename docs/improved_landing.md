## Improved Product Explanation

### What Sharwi Is

Sharwi is the reputation layer for work.

It captures real contributions, verifies them with evidence and company confirmation, and turns them into a portable profile with skills, work history, reviews, and reputation score. Workers use Sharwi to build career capital. Companies use Sharwi to discover credible talent and activate authentic expertise.

### How It Works

1. Capture work
   Connect tools or log achievements manually.
2. Verify proof
   Attach evidence and request verification.
3. Build reputation
   Skills, reviews, and verified work power the Sharwi score.
4. Create visibility
   Sharwi can turn verified work into optional shareable posts.
5. Unlock discovery
   Companies find people through proof, not noise.

## Improved Section Copy

### Problem

The best people often lose to the loudest people.

Most professional value never becomes visible. Resumes are shallow, LinkedIn rewards consistency over substance, and advocacy programs fail because they depend on people manually performing online.

### Worker Reputation Layer

For workers:

- Build a portable profile from real work.
- Separate verified proof from self-claimed experience.
- Receive structured reviews from real collaborators.
- Carry your reputation with you between companies.

### Company Value Layer

For companies:

- Verify real work instead of guessing from resumes.
- Discover workers through trust-backed data.
- Turn internal expertise into authentic external visibility.
- Measure impact with governance and attribution controls.

### Trust and Privacy

- Nothing publishes without user approval.
- Verified work is clearly labeled.
- Private company data stays private.
- Workers control discoverability and profile visibility.
