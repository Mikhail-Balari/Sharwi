# Sharwi Product Analysis

## Product Summary

Sharwi is a proof-backed professional visibility platform. It captures real work activity from integrations and manual updates, transforms that evidence into structured achievements and optional shareable content, and converts it into a portable worker reputation profile that belongs to the worker rather than the employer.

The landing page positions Sharwi as a dual-layer product:

- A personal layer for professionals who want their work to become visible without self-promotion.
- An enterprise layer for companies that want authentic employee advocacy, governance, and measurable business impact.

The embedded Base44 mobile demo reinforces that direction with a mobile-first product including onboarding, authentication, activity capture, post generation, profile editing, a personal visibility dashboard, and a company impact dashboard.

## Problem Statement

The current professional ecosystem rewards visibility theater more than verified contribution:

- Important work often stays invisible.
- Recognition is biased toward people who self-promote well.
- Traditional employee advocacy programs fail because they depend on manual posting behavior.
- Workers lose much of their visible track record when they change jobs.
- Companies struggle to convert internal expertise into trusted external reach, recruiting signal, and attributable pipeline.

Sharwi solves this by making real work legible, portable, and measurable.

## Target Users

### Primary Users

- Individual contributors whose work creates value but is under-documented.
- Managers and team leads who want their teams' work to be visible without forcing content creation.
- Growth, sales, product, engineering, and operations professionals who want a stronger professional reputation.

### Secondary Users

- Founders and executives who want authentic leadership visibility.
- Marketing, HR, and talent teams running advocacy or employer-brand efforts.
- Recruiters and hiring managers who need higher-signal worker discovery than resumes and LinkedIn alone.

## Inferred Platform Definition

Sharwi is not best understood as "another social platform." It is a verified work graph with three connected outputs:

1. A worker-owned reputation profile.
2. A company-facing advocacy and analytics system.
3. A discovery surface where verified workers can be found, trusted, and matched to opportunities.

That interpretation fits both the landing page story and the requested MVP scope for worker profiles, reputation scores, reviews, jobs, and worker discovery.

## Inferred Core Features

### Work Evidence Capture

- Connectors to systems like GitHub, Jira, CRM, docs, and internal systems.
- Manual activity logging for workers without system integrations.
- Structured work evidence attached to achievements.

### AI Transformation Layer

- Turn raw work into achievement summaries and suggested posts.
- Adapt tone by worker profile type: technical, commercial, executive.
- Keep human approval before publishing.

### Worker Reputation Layer

- Portable worker profile with verified work history.
- Skill graph and role metadata.
- Reviews from companies or collaborators.
- Reputation score based on proof, recency, quality, and reviews.

### Company Layer

- Team onboarding and company memberships.
- Governance, approvals, audit trail, and compliance controls.
- Aggregated analytics for participation, reach, and business impact.

### Discovery Layer

- Search and filter workers by skills, role, location, proof, reputation, and industry.
- View verified work history before contacting or hiring.
- Match workers to jobs or shortlists based on proof-backed relevance.

### Feed and Notifications

- Feed of worker achievements, suggested posts, featured talent, and opportunities.
- Notifications for reviews, verification requests, discovery interest, and publishing prompts.

## Inferred User Workflows

### Worker Workflow

1. Sign up and create a worker profile.
2. Connect work sources or log achievements manually.
3. Review generated summaries and optional shareable content.
4. Build work history, skills, reviews, and reputation score over time.
5. Become discoverable to companies and opportunities.

### Company Workflow

1. Create company account and invite team members.
2. Connect internal systems or verify submitted work.
3. Review worker-generated content or evidence when required.
4. Track advocacy and discovery outcomes.
5. Search for high-signal workers and engage them.

### Verification Workflow

1. Worker submits a job or achievement.
2. Sharwi attaches evidence from integrations or documents.
3. Company admin or verifier approves, rejects, or requests more proof.
4. Verified work updates the worker profile and reputation score.

## Base44 Demo Findings

The prototype metadata and bundle expose a mobile-first app with:

- Splash and onboarding flows.
- Authentication.
- Home/activity and generation flows.
- Profile editing and preferences.
- A "My Visibility" dashboard.
- A "Company Impact" dashboard.
- Notifications, posting success states, and tone selection.

This suggests the first mobile MVP should focus on worker experience first, with lightweight company visibility rather than a full admin console in mobile.

## Notes Extracted From The Landing Page

### Messaging

- "Real work deserves real visibility."
- "Sharwi transforms everyday work into visible impact."
- "No vanity metrics. No performance theater."
- "This isn't a social network or another content platform."

### Product Mechanics

- Capture -> Transform -> Amplify -> Measure.
- AI proposes content; the worker approves.
- Portable Sharwi Profile and Badge survive company changes.
- Company value is measured through attribution and CAC reduction.

### Enterprise Claims

- Integrates with GitHub, Jira, Salesforce, ServiceNow, and CRM systems.
- Supports governance, audit trails, and approval workflows.
- Measures visibility using business metrics, not only engagement.

### Strong Signals For MVP Direction

- Worker ownership is a core trust principle.
- Verified history is more defensible than follower count.
- Sharwi should preserve proof, portability, and consent by default.
- The product has room to evolve beyond advocacy into hiring and reputation infrastructure.
